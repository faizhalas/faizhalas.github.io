Given the large number of articles lost due to the journal's closure, the goal of this project is to preserve the content of an article published in an Indonesian library and information journal. It also intends to provide textual data for analysis, as such data is not widely available and requires significant time and resources to collect. The resulting data is textual information stored in tabular format.
